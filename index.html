About Black & Bilingual

You name it.
We speak it.

Black people around the world speak a multitude of languages. Black & Bilingual gives light to that rich, multilingual diaspora.

Founded by Erica “Miss-Enformed” Baker, Black & Bilingual is a cultural education company creating books, interactive learning experiences, multimedia projects, and public scholarship that make language and culture engaging, affirming, and accessible.
