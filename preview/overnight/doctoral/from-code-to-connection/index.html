From Code to Connection

How AAVE speakers acquire Romance languages through identity, linguistic transfer, and cultural resonance.

← Back to Research & Doctoral Coursework

Project overview

This research proposal explores how heritage AAVE speakers learn Spanish, French, and Portuguese, and how dialect awareness and global Black language connections may shape motivation, confidence, comprehension, and engagement.

Why it matters in my work

Rather than positioning Standard English as the unquestioned baseline for language learning, the proposal treats AAVE as a legitimate linguistic system and asks what becomes possible when Black learners can connect their existing language knowledge to global Black Romance-language varieties.
