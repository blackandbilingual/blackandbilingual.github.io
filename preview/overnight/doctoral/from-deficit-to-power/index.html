From Deficit to Power

The Resistant Capital Challenge reframes multilingual students’ knowledge and resistance as forms of cultural wealth rather than deficits.

← Back to Research & Doctoral Coursework

Project overview

Designed as professional development for educators of multilingual students, this project uses resistant capital as a lens for recognizing the knowledge, strategies, and cultural resources students bring into school spaces.

Why it matters in my work

The project reflects a recurring thread in my work: moving away from deficit-based interpretations of students and toward frameworks that recognize culture, language, and lived experience as intellectual assets.
