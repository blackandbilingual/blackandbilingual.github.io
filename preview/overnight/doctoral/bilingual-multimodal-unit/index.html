Bilingual Multimodal Unit Plan

Language, identity, and civic justice for grades 9–10.

← Back to Research & Doctoral Coursework

Project overview

This unit plan was designed for a bilingual social-studies and civic-engagement context. Students analyze how language shapes identity and participation, compare civic voice across cultures, and create a multimodal civic-identity project.

Why it matters in my work

The unit translates theory into curriculum. It reflects my interest in learning environments where multilingual students can maintain and develop their home languages while using language as a tool for voice, advocacy, and community participation.
