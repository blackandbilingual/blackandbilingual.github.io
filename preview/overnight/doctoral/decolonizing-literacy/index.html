Decolonizing Literacy

Challenging English-only norms through methods supporting linguistic justice.

← Back to Research & Doctoral Coursework

Project overview

This focal-point project examines how English-dominant literacy norms can operate as gatekeeping mechanisms for multilingual and multicultural students. It brings linguistic justice, dialect studies, translanguaging, multimodality, and reading theory into one framework.

Why it matters in my work

The project connects directly to Black & Bilingual by asking how literacy can move from a gatekeeping practice toward a more liberatory model that treats students’ full linguistic repertoires as academically legitimate.

Core themes

  • Linguistic Justice
  • Translanguaging
  • Multimodality
  • Culturally Sustaining Pedagogy
