Study Abroad, Motivation & Identity

An exploration of Spanish learning through study abroad, motivation, and the ways identity shapes language-learning experience.

← Back to Research & Doctoral Coursework

Project overview

This project examines how study-abroad experiences can influence second-language motivation, identity, and engagement. It centers the learner’s social and cultural experience rather than treating language acquisition as only a technical process.

Why it matters in my work

It helped sharpen my interest in the relationship between language learning and identity—especially how belonging, cultural connection, and real-world context can change a learner’s willingness to engage.
